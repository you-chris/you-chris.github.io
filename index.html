Hi, I'm Christopher. I build AI agents that help people talk about their health.

What are you working on now?

At the Johns Hopkins Center for Digital Health & AI, I build next-generation AI agents for healthcare and bring them responsibly into clinical practice. Before Hopkins, at the University of Florida, I built AI agents for NIH-funded R24 and U01 projects helping patients learn about and join cancer clinical trials.

About

Postdoctoral Fellow, Center for Digital Health & AI, Johns Hopkins University.

I work on AI agents for health: LLM-driven conversational and multi-agent systems, how people engage with and trust them, and how to deploy them responsibly. Earlier work in virtual reality and embodiment still shapes how I design these interactions. Much of it is collaborative: with partners such as the Mayo Clinic, I've worked on NIH- and NSF-funded projects addressing cancer disparities in underrepresented populations.

I hold a PhD in Human-Centered Computing and an M.S. in Computer Science from the University of Florida.

Outside of research I travel, spend time on the lakes, and (poorly) play golf. Boba, my cat, supervises most of my coding.

I'm always glad to talk about new collaborations in human-computer interaction, AI agents, LLMs, and AR/VR.
